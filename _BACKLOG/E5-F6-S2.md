# E5-F6-S2 Rework automatic Material surface role card assignment across light and dark modes

## User Story

As a designer, I want the `Surface` Material role card to keep its current light/dark behavior, and I want the other Material surface role cards to receive appropriate automatic tones in both light and dark modes so that the preview accurately reflects Material role relationships instead of leaving those other cards effectively unchanged.

## Related Epic

- [E5](./E5.md) - Dynamic tonal color roles epic.

## Related Feature

- [E5-F6](./E5-F6.md) - Surface role tone assignment and customization feature.

## Depends On

- [E5-F6-S1](./E5-F6-S1.md) - Exclude the base-adjacent tone from key strips and assignments.

## Scope

- Revisit the Material surface role mapping rules for the `Surface` Material role, container roles, inverse roles, outline roles, and on-role colors in light and dark mode.
- Keep the `Surface` Material role card's current light/dark behavior unchanged; it remains the anchor card that follows the existing surface tone slider rules.
- Do not change what the Surface color role tab means; this story is about the Material surface role cards generated for any active color role.
- Define how Material surface role cards other than `Surface` derive light and dark values from the corrected key-tone candidate pool.
- Preserve current contrast requirements for on-surface, on-container, inverse-on-surface, outline, and outline-variant roles.
- Document the chosen rule in code comments or nearby tests where it prevents future ambiguity.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Toggling dark mode continues to update the `Surface` Material role card exactly as it does today.
- Toggling dark mode also updates the other applicable Material surface role cards instead of leaving them effectively static.
- The `Surface` Material role card remains the anchor color for the active mode.
- Container, inverse, outline, and on-role Material surface role cards use valid tones from the corrected assignment pool.
- Material surface role cards, hover tooltips, and the app preview all display the same role name and tone number.
- Tests cover light and dark assignments for the `Surface` Material role card and at least three other Material surface role cards.
