# E5-F6 Surface role tone assignment and customization feature

## Feature Statement

As a designer, I want surface role tones to be assigned from the right tonal candidates and optionally customized per theme mode so that Material surface cards remain trustworthy while still allowing deliberate exceptions.

## Related Epic

- [E5](./E5.md) - Dynamic tonal color roles epic.

## Dependency Order

1. [E5-F6-S1](./E5-F6-S1.md) first removes the base-adjacent tone from the candidate strips and automatic assignment pool.
2. [E5-F6-S2](./E5-F6-S2.md) then reworks automatic light/dark assignment for all surface cards using the corrected candidate pool.
3. [E5-F6-S3](./E5-F6-S3.md) splits tone and contrast settings by theme mode for every role.
4. [E5-F6-S4](./E5-F6-S4.md) finally adds manual card customization on top of the corrected automatic model.

## Deliverables

- [E5-F6-S1](./E5-F6-S1.md): Exclude the base-adjacent tone from key strips and assignments.
- [E5-F6-S2](./E5-F6-S2.md): Rework automatic surface-card assignment across light and dark modes.
- [E5-F6-S3](./E5-F6-S3.md): Split surface tone and contrast settings by theme mode for every role.
- [E5-F6-S4](./E5-F6-S4.md): Customize selected surface card tones with the mouse wheel.
