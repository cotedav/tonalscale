# E5-F4-S1 Generalize Material surface role mapping to any palette role

## User Story

As a designer, I want any color role to produce the same surface, outline, inverse, and on-surface mappings so that custom roles can be evaluated consistently.

## Related Epic

- [E5](./E5.md) - Dynamic tonal color roles epic.

## Related Feature

- [E5-F4](./E5-F4.md) - Dynamic role preview and export integration feature.

## Scope

- Keep the existing surface-role tone mapping utility reusable for any role.
- Replace primary-specific mapping branches with role-id and role-label inputs.
- Support per-role tone and contrast settings from the dynamic preview map.
- Keep surface as the app shell's base palette unless a future story changes that behavior.
- Ensure hover tooltips and surface cards can display any role label.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Any role can produce surface, container, inverse, on-surface, outline, and outline-variant mappings.
- Primary behavior remains unchanged after the generic refactor.
- Custom role mappings use that role's generated tonal strip and preview settings.
- Hover tooltips show the correct role label, surface role, tone number, and hex value.
- Unit tests cover mapping for built-in and custom role ids.
