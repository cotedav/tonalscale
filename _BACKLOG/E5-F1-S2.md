# E5-F1-S2 Store per-role surface preview settings generically

## User Story

As a designer, I want each color role to have its own surface tone and contrast settings so that custom roles can be evaluated with the same controls as built-in roles.

## Related Epic

- [E5](./E5.md) - Dynamic tonal color roles epic.

## Related Feature

- [E5-F1](./E5-F1.md) - Dynamic role domain model and store architecture feature.

## Scope

- Replace role-specific preview fields such as `primarySurfaceContrast` with a role-keyed preview settings map.
- Keep global preview settings, such as app preview dark mode, outside the per-role map.
- Provide defaults for any role missing preview settings during import or migration.
- Keep surface tone limits aligned with the current light and dark mode constraints.
- Expose helpers to read and update the active role preview settings without branching on specific role ids.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every role can store independent contrast, light surface tone, and dark surface tone settings.
- Missing per-role preview settings are filled from documented defaults.
- Existing surface and primary preview behavior remains unchanged after migration.
- Controls can bind to active role preview settings through generic getters/setters.
- Unit tests cover independent per-role preview updates and fallback defaults.
