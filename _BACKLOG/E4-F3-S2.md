# E4-F3-S2 Apply surface and primary roles together in the app preview

## User Story

As a designer, I want the preview to retain both surface and primary colors so that editing one role does not remove the other from the interface.

## Related Epic

- [E4](./E4.md) - Multi-role tonal color system epic.

## Related Feature

- [E4-F3](./E4-F3.md) - Combined Material role preview feature.

## Scope

- Continue applying the surface scale to backgrounds, containers, outlines, and on-surface content.
- Apply primary roles to credible interface elements such as primary actions, active navigation, selected controls, emphasis indicators, and primary containers.
- Keep both role mappings active regardless of which editing tab is selected.
- Update only the affected role colors when its gradient changes.
- Preserve app-preview dark mode and surface contrast/tone controls while combining both role families.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The preview simultaneously contains visible surface and primary role examples.
- Switching editing tabs does not change preview colors by itself.
- Editing surface updates surface-driven preview elements while primary-driven elements retain their values.
- Editing primary updates primary-driven preview elements while surface-driven elements retain their values.
- Light and dark preview modes remain readable and use the proper mappings for both role families.
- Component tests cover simultaneous rendering and independent reactive updates.
