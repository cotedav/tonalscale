# E5-F6-S4 Customize selected surface card tones with the mouse wheel

## User Story

As a designer, I want to click a surface card and use the mouse wheel to adjust its tone one stop at a time so that I can fine-tune individual Material roles without disturbing the rest of the automatic surface system.

## Related Epic

- [E5](./E5.md) - Dynamic tonal color roles epic.

## Related Feature

- [E5-F6](./E5-F6.md) - Surface role tone assignment and customization feature.

## Depends On

- [E5-F6-S1](./E5-F6-S1.md) - Exclude the base-adjacent tone from key strips and assignments.
- [E5-F6-S2](./E5-F6-S2.md) - Rework automatic surface-card assignment across light and dark modes.
- [E5-F6-S3](./E5-F6-S3.md) - Split surface tone and contrast settings by theme mode for every role.

## Scope

- Allow clicking a surface card to select it.
- While selected, use mouse wheel input to move that role tone by one available stop per wheel step.
- Persist customization per color role, per surface role, and per light/dark mode.
- Ignore customized roles when Surface Tone or Surface Contrast sliders recalculate automatic assignments.
- Add a subtle customized indicator on any card with a manual override.
- Provide an accessible way to remove a customization and return the role to automatic assignment.
- Deselect the selected card when clicking outside surface cards.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Clicking a surface card selects it, and clicking outside the cards deselects it.
- Mouse wheel over the selected card changes that role by one available tone stop at a time.
- A customized card shows a subtle visual indicator.
- Customized roles do not change when Surface Tone or Surface Contrast sliders are adjusted.
- Removing the customization restores automatic assignment for the current role and current light/dark mode.
- Light-mode and dark-mode customizations are stored independently.
- Tests cover selection, wheel adjustment, slider immunity, uncustomize behavior, and per-mode persistence.
