# E5-F2-S3 Rename, reorder, and delete custom roles

## User Story

As a designer, I want to rename, reorder, and remove custom roles so that my palette stays organized around the roles I actually need.

## Related Epic

- [E5](./E5.md) - Dynamic tonal color roles epic.

## Related Feature

- [E5-F2](./E5-F2.md) - Role management experience feature.

## Scope

- Provide role management controls for renaming custom roles.
- Provide role ordering controls that update the stored role order.
- Allow deletion of custom roles with confirmation or undo protection.
- Prevent deletion of required built-in roles unless a future requirement explicitly allows it.
- Pick a predictable active role after deleting the current custom role.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Custom roles can be renamed with validation for empty or duplicate labels.
- Custom roles can be reordered and the tab order updates immediately.
- Built-in required roles cannot be deleted through the UI.
- Deleting the active custom role selects a neighboring role without errors.
- Tests cover rename validation, reorder persistence, delete protection, and active-role fallback.
