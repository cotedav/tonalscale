# E5-F5-S2 Reorder color roles by dragging tabs

## User Story

As a designer, I want to drag color role tabs to reorder them so that organizing roles feels direct and does not require separate arrow buttons.

## Related Epic

- [E5](./E5.md) - Dynamic tonal color roles epic.

## Related Feature

- [E5-F5](./E5-F5.md) - Role toolbar ergonomics and default Material roles feature.

## Scope

- Add drag-and-drop reordering to the color role tabs.
- Remove the left and right reorder arrow buttons from the role management controls.
- Keep keyboard-accessible reordering available through a clear non-pointer interaction.
- Prevent invalid drops outside the tab list from changing role order.
- Preserve role order in the store, URLs, import/export payloads, and SVG metadata.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Dragging a role tab before or after another tab updates the role order.
- The active role remains selected after it is dragged.
- Built-in and custom roles can be reordered unless a future rule explicitly restricts them.
- Reorder arrow buttons are no longer shown.
- Unit tests cover pointer reordering and keyboard-accessible reordering.
