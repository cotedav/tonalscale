# E5-F6-S3 Split surface tone and contrast settings by theme mode for every role

## User Story

As a designer, I want every color role to have separate light-mode and dark-mode Surface Tone and Surface Contrast settings so that each role can be tuned for both themes without one mode overwriting the other.

## Scope

- Replicate the Surface color role's theme-aware control behavior for Primary, Secondary, Tertiary, Error, and custom color roles.
- Store Surface Tone and Surface Contrast independently for light mode and dark mode for every role.
- Keep the Surface Tone control using the extended scale for non-Surface color roles, from tone 5 through tone 99, instead of limiting it to the Surface color role's light/dark ranges.
- Preserve the existing Surface color role tone limits unless a separate story changes them.
- Update URL, import, export, migration, and reset behavior so per-mode settings round-trip cleanly.
- Ensure the app preview reads the current role's settings for the active preview theme mode.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Changing Surface Tone for a non-Surface role in light mode does not change that role's dark-mode Surface Tone.
- Changing Surface Contrast for a non-Surface role in light mode does not change that role's dark-mode Surface Contrast.
- Switching preview dark mode swaps the active role controls to that mode's stored values.
- Non-Surface roles can use the extended scale from tone 5 through tone 99 for Surface Tone in both light and dark mode.
- Surface color role keeps its existing light/dark Surface Tone limits and behavior.
- URL state, import/export, reset, and legacy migrations preserve sensible values for the new per-mode settings.
- Tests cover at least one built-in accent role and one custom role.
